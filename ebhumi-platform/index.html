BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know.

Is this an official government service?

No. Records, map geometry, workflow states and amounts are samples. They cannot prove ownership or official status.

Will my work be saved?

Applications, evidence and account preferences are saved on this local server. Browser sign-out does not delete them. Keep your server data backed up; cloud storage is not connected.

Can I pay tax or submit a real application?

No. You can prepare local drafts and review sample receipts. Real payments and submissions require authorised integrations.

Does the assistant use a live AI model?

The assistant uses predefined guidance. It does not verify documents or provide legal title opinions.
